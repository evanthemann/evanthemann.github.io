Three things, done well

Good audio

Levels that don't jump around, dialogue you can hear, wind and hum tamed. Bad audio makes people click away faster than anything else.

Good composition

You framed the shots. I pick the ones that actually work, straighten crooked horizons, and punch in or reframe so your eye lands where it should.

Good cuts

Pacing that keeps moving, jokes that land on the beat, and nothing that overstays its welcome. This is where hours of footage become ten good minutes.

What you're getting (and not)

How it works

Four steps. You mostly just watch.

1

We have a quick call.

Who's it for, how long should it be, and what are the moments that have to be in it? Fifteen minutes is usually enough.

2

Send me your footage.

Share a folder online, or mail me the drive or the cards. Phones, GoPros, cameras — whatever you shot on. You get everything back.

3

You watch the first cut.

Tell me what you love and what you'd change. One round of changes is included.

4

You get the finished video.

A high-quality MP4, ready to share or post. You also get the project file (Blender, which is free), so the edit is never locked away with me.

What it costs

It depends on how much footage you have and how long the finished video should be, so I'd rather give you a real number than a guess.

Tell me what you've got and I'll send back a fixed price. No obligation, and nothing starts until you say so.
