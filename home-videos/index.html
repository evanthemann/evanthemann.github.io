Get Started

Got the whole family together?

Pull up the video of all of you from thirty years ago — on the TV, in about ten seconds. Everyone watches it together, right then.

That's the whole idea. Your home videos shouldn't be stuck on a dead laptop, an old hard drive in a closet, or buried somewhere on your phone. They should be easy to find and easy to share, wherever you happen to be.

How it works

Three steps. I do almost all of it.

1

You send me your videos.

Mail me the hard drive, the old laptop, the memory cards — whatever they're on. If that sounds easier, it usually is. We can also do it over the internet if you'd rather. Either way, you get your things back.

2

I organize and label everything.

This is the part nobody wants to do. I sort through it, name things properly, and put it in order — so "Christmas 1994" is actually called Christmas 1994, and you can find it. Then it goes onto a private server I own and take care of.

3

You open an app and watch.

On your phone, your tablet, your computer, or your TV. It looks and works like Netflix, except everything in it is yours. I get it working on your devices myself, and I'll show you how to use it.

Why do it this way?

It's private. Your videos sit on a server I own and look after personally — not on a giant company's computers. Nothing is scanned, sold, advertised against, or fed into AI.

It's backed up. I keep a second copy in a separate location, so a broken drive or a house fire doesn't lose anything. You keep your originals too — my copy is the convenient one, never the only one.

It's actually organized. Labeled, sorted, and searchable. A pile of files named MOV_0472.mp4 isn't a memory you can share. A folder called "Summer at the Lake, 1988" is.

You get a person, not a help desk. I'm the one who sets it up, and I'm the one who picks up the phone when something's confusing. No tickets, no chatbots, no hold music.

It works anywhere. I work with people all over — everything can be handled by mail, phone, and video call. You don't need me in your living room.
